Privacy Policy

Your privacy, respected

Plain-English promise: we collect only what we need to deliver your service, we delete all your data within 24 hours, we never sell your data, and we never ask for your account passwords.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how ResumeAnna ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website or contact us for resume writing and profile-optimization services. By messaging us or placing an order, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us, mainly over WhatsApp or email, so we can prepare your resume or profile guide. This may include:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email).
  • Career information you send us — your existing resume, work history, education, skills and target role or country.
  • Payment reference details (such as a UPI transaction ID) to confirm your order.

We do not ask for, collect or store your LinkedIn, Naukri or other account passwords or login credentials — even if you offer them.

3. How we use your information

Your information is used strictly to deliver and support the service you requested, including to:

  • Write, format and optimize your resume, cover letter or profile guide.
  • Communicate with you about your order, drafts, edits and delivery.
  • Provide support and respond to questions or grievances.

With your consent, we may occasionally send you helpful updates or offers on WhatsApp. You can opt out any time by telling us to stop.

4. Data retention — deleted within 24 hours

All the data you share with us is deleted within 24 hours. Once your order is delivered and your free edit window is closed, we permanently delete your resume, drafts, documents and personal details (name, contact number, email and career information) from our devices, chats and working folders — within 24 hours. We do not archive or back up your files.

Please save your own copy of the delivered Word and PDF files, because we will not be able to resend them once they are deleted. Where a bill has been issued, a minimal billing record (service, amount, date and transaction reference) may be kept only for as long as any tax or record-keeping requirement applies, and is then removed.

5. Bills & payment records

A bill is provided on request for any payment you make to us — advance payment, part payment or full payment. Request it on WhatsApp and we will send it to you on WhatsApp or by email. The bill contains only the service, amount, date and payment reference; we never store your card, UPI PIN or banking credentials.

6. We never sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information or documents to anyone. We do not share your details with third parties except where strictly necessary to run our service (for example, a payment app you use to pay us) or where required by law.

7. How we protect your information

Most of our communication happens over WhatsApp, which uses end-to-end encryption. We limit access to your information to only what's needed to deliver your order, and we take reasonable measures to keep it secure. No method of transmission over the internet is ever 100% secure, but we treat your documents with care and confidentiality.

8. Your account safety

For LinkedIn and Naukri optimization, you always update your own profile using our step-by-step guide. Because we never take your login access, your accounts remain fully in your control at all times.

9. Third-party links

Our website and guides may link to third-party sites (such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Naukri). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites; please review their policies separately.

10. Your rights

You can, at any time, ask us to:

  • Access the information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your details.
  • Delete your data or withdraw your consent, including from promotional messages.

To make any of these requests, just contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

12. Contact & grievances

For any privacy question, request or grievance, email us at resumeanna.in@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp (+91 99663 44220). We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.
