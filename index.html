FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

How do I place an order?
Just tap any WhatsApp button on this site. Your message comes pre-filled — hit send, share a few details or your current resume, and we take it from there. No forms, no sign-ups.
How long does delivery take?
Most resumes and profile guides are delivered within 24–48 hours of confirmation. Need it sooner? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll try our best.
Are the resumes ATS-friendly?
Yes. Every resume is built to pass Applicant Tracking Systems — clean structure, the right keywords, and a format recruiters and software both read easily.
What files will I receive?
You get a fully editable Word file plus a ready-to-send PDF — yours to keep and reuse whenever you like.
What about edits and revisions?
You get 1 free edit within 24 hours of delivery. After that, additional edits are just ₹50 each.
Do you guarantee a job or interview calls?
Honestly, no one truthfully can. We optimize your resume and profile to maximize quality and visibility, but interview calls depend on your experience, the market and recruiter requirements. We focus on giving you the strongest possible start.
Do you need my LinkedIn or Naukri password?
Never. We never ask for your password or login — even if you offer. You update your own profile using our simple step-by-step guide, so your account always stays secure.
Will I get a bill for my payment?
Yes — a bill is given whenever you ask for it, for any payment: advance payment, part payment or full payment. Just request it on WhatsApp and we'll send it across on WhatsApp or by email.
What happens to my data after delivery?
All your data is deleted within 24 hours of delivery — your resume, documents and personal details are permanently removed from our devices and folders. We never store, sell or share them. Do keep your own copy of the delivered files. Read our Privacy Policy.
